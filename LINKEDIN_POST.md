# LinkedIn Post

🏠📊 **Should you buy a home, rent & invest, or become a landlord?**

I built a free, open-source tool to answer this question with real math — not opinions.

**Property vs Portfolio Simulator** lets you model and compare housing strategies side by side with 40+ configurable parameters:

✅ French & Linear mortgages (fixed, variable, mixed rates)
✅ Bank product bonificaciones (nómina, insurance, pension plan)
✅ Spanish IRPF deductions — mortgage interest, depreciation, bad debt, 4-year carry-forward
✅ Landlord scenarios with vacancy, agent fees, and bad tenant modeling
✅ Rent contract regimes with ITP-indexed increases and market resets
✅ Surplus equalization for fair comparison across strategies
✅ Interactive charts + month-by-month CSV export
✅ Full English & Spanish 🇬🇧🇪🇸

The result? A clear, data-driven answer showing your **net worth after taxes** for each strategy over 5-40 years.

Spoiler: The answer depends heavily on your specific numbers. That's exactly why this tool exists.

🔗 Try it free: https://propertyvsportfolio.streamlit.app
💻 Open source: https://github.com/Neogus/property_portfolio_simulator

Built with Python, Streamlit & Plotly. Feedback welcome!
